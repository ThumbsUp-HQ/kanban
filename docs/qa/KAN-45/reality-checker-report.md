   - In [runner.py](file:///home/adamoutler/kanban/agent/gates/runner.py#L71-L76,L91-L96,L134-L139,L199-L204), `poll_once`, `_load_entities`, `record_decision`, and `record_failure` all bind `RLSContext(principal_id=uuid.UUID(int=0), workspace_id=uuid.UUID(int=0), token_scopes=("admin",), aal_level=2)`.
   - Under `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` as `kanban_app` ([p5_17a_workflow_hooks.up.sql](file:///home/adamoutler/kanban/database/migrations/sql/p5_17a_workflow_hooks.up.sql#L115-L178)), `workflow_transition_executions` and `workflow_hook_invocations` `SELECT` policies require `workspace_id = app_current_workspace_id()` AND `app_has_object_capability(issue_id, 1)`, and `issue_comments` `INSERT` requires `author_id = app_current_principal_id()` and capability 2 on `issue_id` (as directly observed in `live-fullstack-proof.txt` lines 20-23).    - [runner.py](file:///home/adamoutler/kanban/agent/gates/runner.py#L208-L276) acquires `with_for_update(skip_locked=True)` on `WorkflowHookInvocation` in `poll_once()` and awaits `self.worker.handle_dispatch(event)` before releasing/committing that transaction. 3. **Medium - `validate_reality_checker_report` `claims_ready` boolean logic and `runner.py` status/deadline mismatches**:
   - In [proof.py](file:///home/adamoutler/kanban/kanban/issues/proof.py#L324-L326), `claims_ready = "READY" in combined_check or bool(re.search(r"\bA\b", cert_body) or ...)` uses `or` rather than requiring `READY` explicitly when `require_ready=True` (so a report with `Grade: A` and `Status: PENDING` without `READY` would pass).
