## Detailed Findings by Model Family & Provider

### 1. Gemma & Granite Local Ollama Models (`gemma3:4b`, `granite3.3:2b`, `gemma4:e2b`)
- **Native JSON Schema (`StrictVerdictSchema`):** All three local edge models (`gemma3:4b`, `granite3.3:2b`, and `gemma4:e2b`) successfully conformed to Ollama's `format` JSON schema constraint generated by `dereference_json_schema(StrictVerdictSchema.model_json_schema())` with zero schema-parse failures.
- **Veto Accuracy on `KAN-2`:** Every model immediately caught the absurd/unverified `"Build a rocket ship and serve ice cream on Saturn"` ticket, returning `decision: "reject"` and citing the impossible scope plus missing design doc, unit tests, and verification proof.
- **Hardware & Cutoff Tuning Observation (`192.168.1.115` vs `192.168.1.101`):**
  - Querying `/api/ps` on `192.168.1.115:11434` showed `qwen3-vl:4b` pinned in VRAM (`expires_at: 2319-03-01`), leaving `size_vram: 0` for `gemma3:4b` (CPU fallback). Consequently, `gemma3:4b` required `31.4s` on the longer `KAN-1` context (hitting the `20,000 ms` cutoff cleanly before succeeding at `35,000 ms`), whereas `gemma4:e2b` on `192.168.1.101` completed `KAN-1` in `19.9s` and `KAN-2` in `5.2s`.
  - When a cold-load or CPU-bound prompt exceeded `timeout_ms`, `GateWorkerRunner` enforced `min(provider.timeout_ms, agent.timeout_ms)` and failed closed (`status: timed_out`, card retained in `In Progress`, veto comment `Gate timed out after 20000 ms` persisted).

### 2. Heavy Reasoning Model Cutoff Verification (`nemotron-3-super:latest`)
- **Strict 5s Cutoff (`timeout_ms = 5000`):** Triggering `KAN-2 -> Done` with `timeout_ms = 5000` aborted cleanly at `6,023 ms` wall time (`executionId: 75b43965-ea98-4dda-8b2f-70108b28c91c`), leaving `KAN-2` safely in `In Progress` and posting `Gate timed out after 5000 ms` (`commentId: 57064d7a-e6c8-4a63-b01f-39e5a5e6c3b1`) with zero hung worker threads.
- **Extended 45s Cutoff (`timeout_ms = 45000`):** Raising `timeout_ms` to `45000` allowed `nemotron-3-super:latest` to complete its `<think>...</think>` reasoning pass (stripped cleanly by `strip_think_tags`), rejecting `KAN-2` in `26,502 ms` (`commentId: 95ff1d33-7ef1-42fe-bf17-f1a47a1eed60`) and approving `KAN-1` in `31,940 ms` (`commentId: 9f913753-4cc9-41ad-8036-9db293479d82`).

### 3. OpenAI (`gpt-4o-mini`)
- Completed `KAN-1` (`approve` -> `Done`, `commentId: 81b352ad-48ae-4e19-ae76-00aef8f61974`) in `3,846 ms` and `KAN-2` (`reject` -> `In Progress`, `commentId: 6cb8fc5f-34a4-4d4f-8d66-572eeb4db6e3`) in `2,224 ms`.
- Zero `sk-proj-` secret material appeared in comments, issue responses, UI DOM, or container logs (`LOG_LEAK_COUNT: 0`).

### 4. Google Gemini (`gemini-2.5-flash-lite` with `AQ.Ab8R...REDACTED`)
- **Model Discovery (`probe_provider`):** Passed cleanly on `/admin/ai`, returning 50 live models (`gemini-2.5-flash-lite`, `gemini-2.5-flash`, `gemini-2.5-pro`, etc.).
- **Gate Execution (`StrictVerdictSchema`):** After patching `agent/providers/gemini.py` and `agent/gates/runner.py` so `AQ.` keys paired with `https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com` use `mode="ai_studio_paid"` (`vertexai=False`) with `api_version="v1beta"`, `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` delivered the **lowest end-to-end latency in the entire benchmark**:
